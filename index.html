Rotation Rules
4 players: Across 6 matches, cycle through all 3 possible team combinations — 2 matches with each pairing.

5 players: One player sits out each match (loser's team rotates one out, sitting player joins the survivor). If a pair wins 2 matches in a row, they split before the 3rd — one stays, one joins the other side, and the surviving loser pairs with whoever's left.

6 players: Split into 3 fixed teams of 2. First ~30 min: round robin — each team plays the other two once, then sits (3 matches total, everyone faces everyone). Second ~30 min: reshuffle into 3 fresh teams with zero repeat pairings from the first half. Use 🎲 Suggest Today's Lineup at the start — it gives you both halves at once, based on this month's pairing history.
